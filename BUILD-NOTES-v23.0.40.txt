Source/research notes:
- Official Wisconsin DNR pages were used as primary sources for all records.
- For normal campgrounds where DNR Get Directions coordinates were visible, those coordinates were used.
- For distributed systems (river camps, flowage camps, backpack shelters, sandbars/islands), a system/area-level pin was intentionally used and documented in locationPrecision/sourceNotes rather than inventing individual campsite pins.
- Where accessible DNR text did not publish individual coordinates, the record says so explicitly instead of pretending the coordinate is an individual campsite coordinate.
