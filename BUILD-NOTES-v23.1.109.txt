Tod's Boondocking & Camping Maps — v23.1.109
Muskallonge duplicate correction + Michigan state-forest/Fox River area resolver repair

Purpose
- Remove the displaced eastern duplicate for Muskallonge Lake State Park Campground.
- Retain the farther-west Muskallonge marker that Tod confirmed is correct.
- Correct the retained Muskallonge record to the Modern Campgrounds layer based on its electric service, shower building and sanitation station.
- Repair the Michigan State Forest Land area resolver so official state-forest ownership parcels have a better chance of drawing around the Fox River/Seney corridor and elsewhere in Michigan.
- Preserve the Fox River Pathway camping-corridor geometry as an explicit lead rather than inventing a route polygon or campsite point.

What was determined
1. Muskallonge Lake
- data/states/MI.js contained two records:
  - s628 at 46.6766473, -85.6280737 (farther west; Tod confirmed correct)
  - s629 at 46.641, -85.428 (displaced eastern duplicate)
- v23.1.109 removes s629.
- s628 is renamed Muskallonge Lake State Park Campground and moved to Modern Campgrounds.

2. Fox River / Seney area coverage
- No dedicated Fox River Pathway outline or legal-camping corridor existed in the runtime area data.
- Worker research identified that as future official route/geometry work, not an already-loaded area that merely failed to render.
- The broader Michigan State Forest Land ownership overlay was intended to draw official state-forest parcels at zoom 6+, but its ArcGIS child-item discovery remained too brittle.
- v23.1.109 improves child-item discovery without returning to the unbounded item-ID scan that caused the earlier loading delay.

ArcGIS resolver changes
- Try both the configured Michigan DNR ArcGIS portal sharing endpoint and the public ArcGIS Online sharing endpoint.
- Recognize more legitimate ArcGIS item-reference keys used by Web AppBuilder and Experience Builder configurations.
- Extract item IDs from ArcGIS item URLs and id/itemid/webmap query parameters.
- Parse embedded JSON configuration strings when they contain nested map/service references.
- Keep item discovery bounded; arbitrary unrelated 32-character strings are still not treated as item IDs.
- Keep fail-closed behavior: no guessed layer or fake geometry is drawn if no safe official polygon layer resolves.
- Increase Michigan state-forest parcel opacity/line visibility so successfully loaded ownership parcels are distinguishable on normal basemaps.

Fox River Pathway lead handling
- Added one durable data/leads.js record for official Fox River Pathway route/legal-area geometry rescue.
- No point, rectangle, centroid or hand-drawn corridor was added.
- Future work must obtain an official pathway polyline or defensibly georeference the official map, intersect it with official state-forest ownership, and apply campground/refuge/private/posting exclusions.
