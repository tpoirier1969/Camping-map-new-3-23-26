## Data philosophy applied
- These are travel-center/truck-stop parking-lot records, not campgrounds or RV parks.
- All new records use layer = overnight-parking and siteForm = parking-lot.
- overnightParking remains Limited, because even where official pages support overnight/truck/RV parking, travelers still need to obey posted signs, use designated spaces, and avoid blocking truck operations.
- Some source language is truck-driver-focused; passenger-car, van, and RV use should be treated as likely but still subject to site rules and staff direction.

## Precision note
- Pilot/Flying J/ONE9 official pages include coordinates.
- Love's and TA records use official address/operator pages plus address-level or public truck-stop-directory coordinate support where available. They are property-level pins, not hand-selected exact overnight stalls.
