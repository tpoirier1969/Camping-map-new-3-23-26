## Data philosophy applied
- Overnight Parking remains parking-lot-style only.
- No RV parks/campgrounds were added to Overnight Parking.
- Cracker Barrel entries are marked Limited because current reporting says overnight parking is location-by-location and staff/manager dependent.
- Walmart entries are marked Limited because permission is store-by-store and depends on management, posted rules, local ordinances, and property constraints.
- Locations without store-specific overnight approval are candidates, not guarantees.

## Important QA note
- Several newly added coordinates are business/address-level points and should be checked against aerial imagery for exact parking-lot placement before treating them as final pin placement.
- This is still better than marking permission as Allowed. The layer now gives travelers practical candidate points while clearly warning them to call/check signs.
