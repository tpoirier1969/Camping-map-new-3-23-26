## New in v23.0.27
- The Near Me button now does more than zoom to the browser location.
- On phone or desktop, Near Me asks for browser location permission, selects mapped states intersecting roughly a 200-mile radius around the user, loads those states, places a user marker, and zooms to the 200-mile area.
- The radius is intentionally approximate and state-based for loading. The app loads nearby mapped state data, not a precise clipped circular data extract.
- If no nearby mapped state is found, it falls back to Michigan.
