Tod's Boondocking & Camping Maps
v23.1.26 — UI control-home cleanup

Baseline:
- Full live repo/package ZIP: Camping-map-new-3-23-26-main.zip
- v23.1.25 fixed-files overlay applied first.

Purpose:
- Clean up the control interface without changing campsite data.
- Keep v23.1.25 single-version architecture intact.

Changed behavior:
- Sidebar controls are now split into five unique homes:
  1. Options
  2. Where
  3. Route
  4. Layers
  5. Filters
- Each home opens as its own panel; panels no longer scroll down into each other.
- Desktop sidebar has a five-button home navigation row.
- Phone bottom bar now has Map, Options, Where, Route, Layers, and Filters.
- Route controls were moved out of Find/Near Me and into the Route home.
- Account, map type, add-site tools, manual queue, mobile legend, and information/reference live in Options.
- State selection and Find/Near Me live in Where.
- Layer visibility and Needs Verification live in Layers.
- Filters contain cost, waterfront, access, and quick-filter controls.

Official Area Outlines:
- Simplified from a per-state dropdown/control cluster into a normal on/off layer.
- The new Official Area Outlines toggle shows all official context outlines for the active states.
- Changing active states refreshes the shown outlines when the toggle is on.
- Fit shown outlines remains available.
- Turning outlines off clears all outline layers.

Data impact:
- No campsite data changed.
- No state manifest changed.
- No record counts changed.
- No layer assignments changed.
- No coordinate changes.

Changed files:
- index.html
- app.js
- version.js

Intentionally not changed:
- data/states-manifest.js
- data/states/*.js
- data/supplements/*.js
- config.js
- CSS/icon assets
- Montana data/workflow
