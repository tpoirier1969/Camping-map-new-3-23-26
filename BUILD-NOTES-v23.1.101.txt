# Build Notes — v23.1.101 Federal Areas de-duplication

Purpose:
- Fix Federal Areas drawing duplicate forest outlines in different styles/colors.
- Keep the layer clean and sparse: detailed official federal outline/surface context wins; broad USFS administrative boundary only fills gaps.

Changed files:
- app.js
- version.js
- index.html
- PROJECT_RULES.md
- Project Rules.md

Behavior change:
- Federal Areas now filters redundant federal area outline records before loading.
- For National Forests with an existing detailed USFS-owned surface/ownership outline, the older broad administrative forest outline is not auto-loaded.
- Non-FS/private-inholding context polygons are not auto-loaded as part of Federal Areas because they are not federal areas and were creating a second-color duplicate-looking layer.
- The dynamic USFS administrative boundary overlay now uses a stronger normalized forest/unit key, so it is suppressed when a matching detailed federal outline is already available.
- Wilderness/backcountry rule areas remain allowed as distinct nested federal rule areas.

No data changes:
- No campsite records changed.
- No leads or rejected records changed.
- No new discovery or promotion work.
- No config.js.
