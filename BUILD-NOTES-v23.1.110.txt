Tod's Boondocking & Camping Maps — v23.1.110
Area-overlay zoom warning popup

Purpose
- Prevent users from turning on Federal Areas or State / Local Areas at a zoom level where zoom-gated geometry is intentionally hidden and assuming the layer is broken or empty.
- Explain the exact zoom threshold without changing the selected layer or automatically moving the map.

Behavior added
- When Federal Areas is explicitly enabled below a relevant display threshold, a modal popup opens immediately.
- When State / Local Areas is explicitly enabled below a relevant display threshold, the same popup opens immediately.
- When both area systems are enabled together, the popup combines the applicable thresholds.
- The warning is generated from the selected states and the actual `minZoom` values on applicable area records.
- Broad U.S. Forest Service boundaries use the existing zoom-5 threshold.
- Michigan State Forest Land uses its existing zoom-6 threshold.
- The popup states that the layer remains enabled and that the hidden geometry loads automatically after zooming in.
- It also explains that area records without a zoom limit may still appear.

Design decisions
- No automatic zoom was added. State/layer selection remains a data-display control and does not unexpectedly move the map.
- The warning appears only for an explicit user area-layer selection, not on startup and not repeatedly during normal zooming.
- The existing loading lifecycle, area-source resolver, geometry, colors, layer meanings and campground data were not changed.
